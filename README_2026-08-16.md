## Three things to read before implementing

**1. A defect blocks one canonization.** The grammar experiment (B10) records
whether a take rode the grammar and no screen says so. Facts about an image
ride on the image — and without it the experiment cannot be evaluated, which
is the only reason the switch exists. That row does not leave the table until
the take's fact line carries `GRAMMAR — <NAME>`.

**2. One direction is reversed.** C1 ratifies brief/camera verbs losing their
ghost boxes to §1.4, which reverses the user's 2026-08-13 direction. Flagged,
not buried: if the boxes are wanted back, they return as **tools in a bar**
(A7), not as boxes on inline verbs.

**3. The arrange room is still un-ruled** and has now been deferred twice
for the same reason — it has never been recorded, and its open items (chip
sizes, scrim strength, snap values, claim arrows) are feel, not description.
It is the first item of the next recording walk. If it is not in the next
bundle, the queue cannot clear it and that should be said out loud.
