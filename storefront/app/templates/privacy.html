PRIVACY

What we store, and what we never see.

What we store

When you buy, we store your email address, what you bought, and Stripe's identifiers for the transaction — that's the whole customer record. Card details never touch our servers; payment runs entirely on Stripe (their privacy policy).

Your creative work

Download: everything lives on your machine. Nothing is uploaded to us — the only network calls are the render requests you make to the engine providers with your own keys. Cloud: your workspace's data lives on its own private volume, gated by your access token. We don't read it, mine it, or train on it; it exists to serve your workspace and is removed per the retention policy after cancellation.

What we don't do

No analytics trackers, no ad pixels, no selling or sharing of your data, no marketing mail. The only email we send is transactional — your licenses, at your request.

Your rights

Want your purchase record corrected or deleted (where the law lets us delete transaction records)? Reply to your receipt email and we'll do it.
