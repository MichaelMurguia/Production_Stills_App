01

Screenplay

The draft is read end to end — not skimmed for keywords. It comes back with the film's design languages, its environments, its locations, its cast, and an honest list of what the script never settles.

THE GATE

Everything downstream cites this file. Replace the draft and existing work keeps its citations — anything broken surfaces for review.

WHAT PROPOSED MEANS

The read proposed nine design languages; three were duplicates or noise. It proposes; it never adds itself. You confirm, rename, or drop.

02

Production design

You set the look once, in one document. Style plates are given narrow jurisdictions — a cinematography plate governs contrast and tonal depth, and is explicitly forbidden from dictating a scene's hour or hue. Every render afterwards obeys the bible.

ART DIRECTION BIBLE · LOOKBOOK3 PLATES SET

Cinematography

CONTROLS contrast · tonal depth · naturalism

NEVER a panel's hour or hue — set dawn, dusk and night separately

DESIGN LANGUAGES SET BY THIS BIBLE

POST-FALL FRONTIER GRM ORDER BELTMINER & TERRA NOVA GENETICS SKINNERS RESISTANCE

THE GATE

No bible, no consistent look. Every prompt the app compiles draws its rendering language from this document — you never retype a style note.

WHY JURISDICTIONS

A reference left unbounded leaks. Naming what a plate does not control is what keeps a dusk plate from turning every scene to dusk.

03

Breakdown

The script breakdown, done in software. Each scene is decomposed by element — set, set dressing, props, picture vehicles, atmosphere — and every element carries the line of script that permits it. Nothing enters a render that can't say where it came from.

THE GATE

A sheet can't lock while a required element lacks evidence — and only a locked sheet can render. You see the unmet condition before you spend anything.

WHAT HOLD MEANS

The read inferred it. It is never allowed to promote its own guess — you accept the inference or cut the element.

04

Panels

Concept panels render against the approved references and are judged one at a time, at full size, with their provenance beside them. A rejection isn't lost work — its reason rides along on every future take of that panel.

THE GATE

A take is a candidate, never canon. Nothing reaches a board — or the reference library — without your approval.

NATIVE RESOLUTION

Renders are never upressed. A panel smaller than its slot is flagged and regenerated larger, not stretched.

05

Board

Approved panels compose onto a true 4K presentation board. Titles, panel captions and the canon block are drawn by the app — never by the model — so the type is always correct and always legible at print size.

BOARD-0001 — KYRA COSTUME & ACTION STUDY 5 PANELS 3840×2160TYPOGRAPHY DRAWN BY THE APP, NEVER THE MODEL

THE GATE

Every slot must read OK — approved, and large enough for its position — before the board will assemble.

THE LOOP CLOSES

An approved panel can be promoted back into the reference library, so the next scene anchors to work you already signed off on.
